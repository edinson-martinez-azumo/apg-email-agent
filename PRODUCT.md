# Product

## Register

product

## Users

APG (APackaging Group) sales reps — small internal team in Azusa, California. Desktop-first usage in an office environment during business hours. Primary job: respond to customer inquiries about cosmetic/personal care packaging (bottles, pumps, tubes, jars) without spending time on repetitive email drafting.

## Product Purpose

Internal AI-powered email triage tool. Ingests customer emails from Gmail, generates draft replies grounded in APG's product catalog (SKUs, MOQ, pricing), and lets reps review, edit, approve, and send the reply in one workflow. Success = fewer minutes per inquiry, no wrong products quoted, consistent professional tone.

## Brand Personality

Professional, efficient, trustworthy. This is a back-office tool for a B2B manufacturing company — not a consumer app. The interface should feel like a well-made internal dashboard: calm, dense-enough, zero decoration for its own sake.

## Anti-references

- Splashy SaaS marketing dashboards (Stripe's own marketing site, Intercom's UI teardowns)
- Dark neon / cyberpunk aesthetics
- Gradient-heavy "AI product" landing pages
- Consumer social app patterns (Instagram-style cards, story rings, excessive animation)

## Design Principles

1. **Serve the task, not the brand.** Every pixel should help a rep process an email faster. Decoration that doesn't earn its place is debt.
2. **Scannable over beautiful.** Reps glance at 20+ emails a day. Hierarchy, contrast, and truncation matter more than visual flair.
3. **Trust through precision.** SKU numbers, sender names, status labels — show exact data. Round numbers, vague states, or hidden info erode trust.
4. **Calm under load.** Loading states, errors, and edge cases should feel handled, not alarming. Calm visual weight throughout.
5. **Desktop-first with mobile tolerance.** Primary use is desktop (office). Mobile should not break but need not be optimized.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Internal tool, but keyboard navigation and screen-reader support matter for inclusion. No known specific user needs beyond standard baseline.
